## Factor Analysis

Spearman was also influenced by Galton’s ideas of measuring individual differences in human abilities and by his early ideas on intelligence testing. Using Pearson’s product-moment correlation and the principal components method[\*](./086-Factor_Analysis.md#rfn9) that Pearson introduced in 1901, Spearman created a new statistical method, known as **factor analysis**, which reduces a set of complex data into a more manageable form that makes it possible to detect structures in the relationship between variables.

I went on to create the first psychometric theory of intelligence with my two-factor theory, which measures general and specific abilities.

[\*](./086-Factor_Analysis.md#fn9) A general statistical procedure for finding an efficient representation of a set of correlated data.
