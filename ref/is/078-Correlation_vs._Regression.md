## Correlation vs. Regression

Though Galton wanted to measure the correlation of stature between father and son, Pearson discovered in 1896 that Galton’s procedure for finding “co-relation”, as he spelt it, measured the _slope_ of the regression line, which was a measure of the regression coefficient instead.

![image](./images/00201.jpeg)

Galton fitted an arbitrary line and then tested to see if the slope of the line were 1\. If the line produced a value of 1, this would have meant that the predicted height of the children was identical to the parent; if the value were less than 1, the children would have been closer to the mean and thus had more moderate heights.
