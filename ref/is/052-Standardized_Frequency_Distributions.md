## Standardized Frequency Distributions

Pearson recognized that there had to be other ways to manage unwieldy data. He developed a systematic way to handle very large data sets by introducing the means to construct **standardized frequency distribution**. This allowed comparisons and generalizations about data sets that had previously been impossible to make.

![image](./images/00115.jpeg)

The basic data-management procedures that Pearson introduced and the statistical methods he devised form the foundations of elementary mathematical statistics.
