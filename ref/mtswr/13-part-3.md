#  The Makeover: Adjusting the Appearance 

This part of the book focuses on techniques to process time series for analysis. To extract information properly, a time series may need to be processed through multiple adjustments and transformations. Decomposition is a crucial step when analyzing time series by breaking it down into its components. Feature extraction is another perspective for extracting information from an observed series. Smoothing and filtering techniques remove noise from a time series to unmask the underlying characteristics.
