#  The Crystal Ball: Forecasting 

Forecasting, the core use of a time series, is covered in this part. The chapters discuss a wide variety of forecasting techniques that range from simple methods to specialized algorithms. The contents are suitable for beginners to advanced professionals. We believe the forecasting methodologies covered in this part will be sufficient to solve the majority of business forecasting problems.
