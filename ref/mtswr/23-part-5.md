#  Part 5 

#  Plot Twists and Turning Points: Measuring Impact and Detecting Anomalies 

It is common to have auto-correlated data in many business scenarios. Standard causal impact measurement methodologies may become unreliable if such inter-dependencies are not properly handled. In this part, we address this problem and present time-series-based techniques for causal impact measurement. Anomalies and change point detection are two crucial phenomena that are observed frequently in business settings. We discuss some methodologies to detect anomalies or change points in the data and how to handle them. This part of the book is suitable for intermediate to advanced professionals.
