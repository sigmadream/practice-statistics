## Statistical Results for Guinness

When Gosset analysed the barley plots using his newly devised z-ratio, he found that the best barley for Guinness was the Archer variety. Once Guinness knew which barley to buy, they wanted to grow it all over Ireland.

![image](./images/00250.jpeg)

We discovered that 1,000 seeds of pure-line Danish Archer barley were available, and these were distributed to all the chosen farmers. With Gosset’s new statistical test it became possible to determine with precision the relative importance of the many factors influencing the quality at different stages of the complicated process of brewing.

Student’s z-ratio became the first statistical test for quality control in industry. Gosset’s ideas, which demonstrated that it was important to determine the quality control of a product, influenced a new generation of statisticians, including R.A. Fisher, Walter Shewart (1891–1967) and W. Edwards Deming (1900–93).
