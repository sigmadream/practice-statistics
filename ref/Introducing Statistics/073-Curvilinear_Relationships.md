## Curvilinear Relationships

Though the numerical index provides some information about the degree of a linear relationship, a scatter plot is a useful tool, because it may reveal instead a curvilinear relationship. Pearson introduced the correlation ratio in 1905 to measure a curvilinear relationship.

An age over lifetime growth curve is curvilinear, whereas an early childhood growth curve is linear. Infants continue to grow until adolescence: they get taller, their hair grows, and they also become more dextrous, agile and flexible. But the lifespan is curvilinear because a number of these characteristics diminish among the elderly: many become shorter, men in particular have a tendency to lose their hair and some become bald, and many others become less agile and flexible as they get older.
