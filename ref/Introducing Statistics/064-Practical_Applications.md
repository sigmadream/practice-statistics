## Practical Applications

This method remains a very widely used tool for industry, marketing and economics. Wool manufacturers use the coefficient of variation to calculate the variation in fibre diameter distribution and yarn irregularities.

![image](./images/00168.jpeg)

The values obtained measure the standards of uniformity of the fibre diameter (poor, average or excellent)…

![image](./images/00169.jpeg)

… Which is the most important factor to describe critical levels of comfort and the physical characteristics of the finished fabric.

This information, for example, enables manufacturers to produce various qualities of wool, depending on the demands of the market.
