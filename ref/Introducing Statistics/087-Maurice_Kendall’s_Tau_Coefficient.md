## Maurice Kendall’s Tau Coefficient

The English statistician **Maurice Kendall** (1907–83) created another ranking method of correlation in 1938, known as Kendall’s tau. This method is a scheme based on the number of agreements or disagreements in ranked data.

![image](./images/00228.jpeg)

It represents a probability, and is the difference between the probability that in the observed data… …the two variables are in the same order versus the probability that the two variables are in different orders.

Kendall’s tau is often used for samples larger than those used for the Spearman rho.
